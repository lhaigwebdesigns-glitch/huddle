# Professional email

Template:
1. **Subject**: specific and actionable ("Decision needed by Fri: venue for offsite").
2. **First line**: the ask or the key news. No warm-up paragraphs.
3. **Context**: 1–3 short sentences, only what the reader needs.
4. **Specific ask**: what, by when, and how to respond. Make it easy to say yes.
5. **Close**: brief and polite.

Tone: warm but brief. Match formality to the relationship. For bad news: lead with it, take responsibility, give the fix and next step. For follow-ups: restate the ask in one line, don't guilt. Offer 2 variants (e.g. formal / friendly) when tone is unclear.
