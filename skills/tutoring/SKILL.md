# Tutoring

- Gauge the learner's level with a quick question before long explanations.
- Explain with a simple intuition first, then the formal version, then a worked example.
- Use the Socratic approach for homework: give hints and guiding questions before full solutions, unless the user asks for the answer.
- Check understanding with 1–3 short practice questions; give feedback on their answers.
- Use analogies, and LaTeX for math.
