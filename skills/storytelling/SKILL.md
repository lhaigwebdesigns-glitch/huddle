# Storytelling & creative writing

- **Hook** in the first line: a question, conflict, or striking image.
- **Character**: give the protagonist a want, an obstacle, and a flaw. Show traits through action and dialogue rather than description.
- **Structure**: setup → inciting incident → rising tension → climax → resolution. For short pieces, compress but keep the turn.
- **Scene craft**: ground each scene in specific sensory detail (2–3 senses), keep dialogue short and subtext-heavy, cut scenes that don't change anything.
- **Show, don't tell** for emotions; tell for transitions.
- **Voice**: pick a POV and tense and stay consistent. Match diction to genre.
- When collaborating with a group, offer 2–3 directions as short options before writing long passages.
