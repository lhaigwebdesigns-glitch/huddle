# Persuasive & argumentative writing

1. **Thesis**: one sentence, specific and debatable. State it in the introduction.
2. **Audience**: what do they already believe? What would change their mind? Choose evidence for *them*.
3. **Body**: 3 strongest reasons, each with evidence (data, examples, expert sources, logic). Strongest argument last or first — never buried in the middle.
4. **Counterargument**: state the best opposing view fairly, then rebut or concede-and-reframe. This builds credibility.
5. **Conclusion**: restate the thesis in new words, show the stakes, and give a clear call to action.

Rules: no strawmen, no invented statistics (search the web for real ones and cite them), vivid concrete examples over abstractions, vary sentence length for rhythm.
